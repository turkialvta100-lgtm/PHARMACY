Find answers to common questions about our pharmacy services?

+
We're open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Holiday hours may vary.

Do you accept insurance?

+
Yes, we accept most major insurance plans. Please bring your insurance card with you, and our team will verify your coverage and process your prescription accordingly.

Do you offer delivery services?

+
Yes, we offer free local delivery for prescriptions within a 5-mile radius. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 2:00 PM. Contact us to schedule your delivery.

How long does delivery take?

+
Delivery usually takes 24–72 hours depending on your location.

Do I need a prescription to buy medicines?

+
Some medicines require a valid prescription, while others are available over the counter. Always check the product description.
